How the work
gets made.

Four phases, one team, no surprises. Most projects run eight to fourteen weeks from kickoff to launch.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Week 1–2

    Workshops, audits and a lot of questions. We leave with a one-page brief everyone signs off on.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Week 2–5

    Identity, structure and a high-fidelity prototype you can click through on your own phone.

  3. 03

    Build

    Week 5–11

    Hand-coded, reviewed weekly on a live staging link. Content goes in early, not the night before.

  4. 04

    Launch & tend

    Week 12 →

    We ship, measure and keep tuning. Search, speed and the small details that compound.
